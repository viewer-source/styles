Glyph growth when state variables are added

A macromolecule of 80 by 40 with state variables added one at a time (empty, 18 px each, 2 px apart). The newest one is in colour; a frame in colour marks an add that changed the size.

Up to 12 state variables all three rows are the same: 12 places fit round an 80 by 40 glyph, 20 px apart along its border, the corners included. The rows differ from the 13th on. A side gains a place only every 20 px of length, so no rule can grow the glyph by less than 20 px in one direction; the rules differ in how large each step is and where it goes.

State variables: 1

Now: places fixed 20 px apart, the glyph grown until a new place is free

The icons never move, so a new place opens only when a whole place fits between two icons already there: 80 to 120 in one add, then 120 by 40 to 160 by 60 in one add.

A: steady, all directions, 2:1 kept

The icons on a side are spread evenly between its corners, so they slide a few px when it grows. A side gains a place every 20 px, so the glyph grows in small equal steps, width plus 20 and height plus 10, one step for about two adds.

B: steady, wider first, then taller back to 2:1, then both

The same even spread and 20 px steps, given first to the width only (height stays 40, as the glyphs beside it) up to the threshold; then the height, until it is at least half the width; then the width again, the height following each time it falls below half.
